Overview

Understanding your skin concern

Every skin condition is different

Skin conditions may affect skin tone, texture, sensitivity, hydration, pigmentation, or overall appearance. Some concerns are temporary, while others may require long-term management and medically guided care. This resource is designed to help you better understand common skin-related concerns frequently associated with pigmentation and aesthetic dermatology.

Melasma

A chronic pigmentation condition commonly appearing as brown or gray-brown patches on sun-exposed areas of the face.

Common symptoms
  • Uneven facial pigmentation
  • Brown patches on cheeks, forehead, or upper lip
  • Pigmentation that worsens with sun exposure
Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation

Darkened areas that appear after skin inflammation, acne, irritation, or injury.

Common symptoms
  • Dark marks after acne or irritation
  • Uneven skin tone
  • Persistent discoloration after inflammation
Sensitive Skin

A skin condition characterized by increased reactivity to skincare products, environmental exposure, or treatments.

Common symptoms
  • Redness
  • Burning or stinging sensation
  • Dryness or irritation
Skin Barrier Damage

A weakened skin barrier may increase sensitivity, dryness, irritation, and discomfort.

Common symptoms
  • Tight or dry skin
  • Flaking
  • Increased sensitivity
Acne & Acne Marks

Acne may lead to inflammation, scarring, or pigmentation changes that affect overall skin appearance.

Common symptoms
  • Active breakouts
  • Red or dark acne marks
  • Uneven texture or inflammation
Uneven Skin Tone

Differences in pigmentation that may result from sun exposure, inflammation, or skin sensitivity.

Common symptoms
  • Patchy complexion
  • Dull appearance
  • Irregular pigmentation

Professional evaluation

"Consider seeking professional evaluation if you experience persistent pigmentation changes, worsening skin sensitivity, or concerns affecting daily confidence."
