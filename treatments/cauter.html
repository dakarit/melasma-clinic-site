Cauter

Electrocautery for superficial skin lesions

Cauter uses controlled electrical current (electrocautery) to precisely remove benign superficial skin lesions such as skin tags, milia, small warts, and sebaceous hyperplasia. The procedure is quick and targeted, with minimal damage to surrounding skin.

Local anaesthetic is applied where necessary. Mild scabbing is expected during the healing period of approximately 7–14 days.

Photo
Key points
  • Removes skin tags, milia, small warts, and similar lesions
  • Precise removal with minimal surrounding tissue impact
  • Quick procedure; local anaesthetic applied as needed
  • Mild scabbing during healing (approximately 7–14 days)
