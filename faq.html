Consultation & Appointment
Do I need an appointment before visiting?
Appointments are highly recommended to help ensure consultation availability and personalized scheduling. However, walk-in visits are also welcome during operational hours.
Can I have a consultation without treatment on the same day?
Yes. You may begin with consultation and skin evaluation before deciding on treatment.
How long does a consultation usually take?
Consultation duration may vary depending on skin condition and treatment planning needs.
Can I choose my preferred doctor?
Yes. However, doctor availability may vary depending on clinic schedule and appointment availability.
Is online consultation available?
Online consultation is currently not available. However, online consultation services may become available in the future depending on clinic policy and operational development.
Treatments
Can men do treatments?
Yes. Our treatments are available for both men and women, depending on individual skin condition and treatment goals.
Are treatments personalized?
Yes. Treatment recommendations are tailored individually based on skin condition, lifestyle, medical evaluation, and your goals.
How do I know which treatment is suitable for me?
A personalized consultation and skin evaluation will help determine the most appropriate treatment approach.
Are treatments painful?
Comfort levels may vary depending on the procedure and individual sensitivity. Our team always prioritizes patient comfort throughout the treatment experience.
How many sessions are usually needed?
Treatment frequency depends on skin condition, treatment goals, and individualized planning.
Can treatments be combined?
Some treatments may be combined depending on skin condition and medical evaluation.
Melasma & Skin Conditions
What causes melasma?
Melasma is influenced by multiple factors including sun exposure, hormones, genetics, heat, and skin irritation. Visit our Melasma resource page to learn more.
Can melasma recur?
Yes. Melasma is often considered a long-term pigmentation condition that may recur depending on triggers and skin condition.
Does sunscreen help melasma?
Consistent sun protection is considered an important part of melasma management.
Can sensitive skin receive treatment?
Treatment suitability depends on skin condition and medical evaluation. We recommend consulting with our doctor to help determine the most appropriate approach for your skin.
Why is skin barrier health important?
A healthy skin barrier may help support skin comfort, reduce irritation, and maintain overall skin condition.
Downtime & Recovery
Is there downtime after treatment?
Downtime varies depending on the procedure performed.
Can I return to work after treatment?
Some treatments allow immediate return to daily activities, while others may require temporary recovery time.
Can I wear makeup after treatment?
This depends on the type of treatment received and post-care recommendations.
Are there post-treatment instructions?
Yes. Post-treatment care instructions will be provided based on the procedure performed.
Payment & Policy
What payment methods are accepted?
We currently support cashless payment methods only. Payments via card and QR-based payment systems are available.
Are treatments refundable?
Refunds are not available, but treatments may be adjusted or changed to other suitable treatments based on clinic policy.
Can I reschedule my appointment?
Yes. Appointment rescheduling is subject to clinic scheduling availability and policy.

Is GEUT suitable for sensitive skin?
GEUT products are formulated with long-term skin barrier-focused ingredients, but suitability may vary individually. For more information, visit our treatments section.
Can GEUT be used together with treatments?
Product recommendations may be adjusted depending on treatment planning and skin condition.
Can GEUT be used daily?
GEUT products are designed for long-term daily skincare maintenance. However, product recommendations may vary depending on individual skin condition and doctor evaluation.
Your Experience
Is the clinic only for melasma treatment?
No. The clinic also provides aesthetic dermatology and skin quality-focused treatments. Please visit our Treatments section for more detailed information.
Are treatments performed by medical professionals?
Yes. Treatments are performed following medical evaluation and clinic protocols, and all procedures are performed directly by doctors.
What should I prepare before consultation?
Patients are encouraged to arrive with minimal makeup and provide relevant skincare or treatment history if available.
