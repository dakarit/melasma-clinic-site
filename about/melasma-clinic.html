About Melasma Clinic

Indonesia's First
Melasma-Focused Clinic

Melasma Clinic by dr. Tompi is a specialized clinic under the Beyoutiful Group, focused on medically-based melasma and pigmentation care that prioritizes skin health and protects the integrity of the skin barrier.

We believe healthy skin should never come at the cost of damaging the skin barrier. That is why our approach combines medical-based treatments, responsible innovation, and comprehensive long-term care designed to help patients manage melasma safely, naturally, and sustainably.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision & Mission

Redefining melasma care in Indonesia

At Melasma Clinic by dr. Tompi, our mission is built on five core principles that guide every treatment decision, patient interaction, and clinical advancement we pursue.

Our Vision

"To become the leading melasma clinic in Indonesia by providing safe, medical-based skin care that respects, protects, and preserves the skin barrier."

Care designed with purpose and responsibility

01

Evidence-Based Medical Care

Providing scientifically grounded melasma and pigmentation treatments under direct doctor supervision.

02

Skin Barrier Education

Helping patients understand the difference between healthy skin and skin damaged by overtreatment, while promoting the importance of skin barrier protection.

03

Responsible Technology

Utilizing modern dermatology technologies that support natural, sustainable results without compromising long-term skin health.

04

Professional & Transparent Experience

Delivering personalized care that is professional, honest, and tailored to each patient's actual skin needs — without excessive procedures.

05

Comprehensive Recovery Approach

Supporting patients with compromised skin barriers or histories of aggressive treatments through restorative and comprehensive care strategies.
