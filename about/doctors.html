Meet the Doctors

At Melasma Clinic by dr. Tompi, our doctors are committed to delivering medical-based melasma and skin care that prioritizes long-term skin health, patient safety, and the protection of the skin barrier.

Every patient's skin condition is unique. That is why our doctors take a personalized and comprehensive approach — carefully understanding each patient's concerns, skin history, and treatment goals before creating tailored care plans designed to support healthier and more sustainable outcomes.

Our approach

"Every treatment decision is made by a qualified doctor — personalized to you, grounded in evidence, and focused on your long-term skin health."
01

Doctor-led care

All procedures and treatment plans at Melasma Clinic are carried out directly by qualified doctors — never delegated without medical oversight.

02

Personalized plans

Our doctors take the time to understand your skin history, concerns, and goals before recommending any treatment — because no two skin conditions are the same.

03

Long-term focus

We measure success by how your skin improves over time — not by the number of procedures. Our doctors are partners in your skin journey, not one-time providers.
